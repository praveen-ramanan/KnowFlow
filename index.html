Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the KnowFlow briefing engine.

Where does the data come from?
KnowFlow connects to premium RSS feeds, developer news boards (like Hacker News), academic archives (arXiv), trending GitHub networks, and industry blog updates.
How does the personalization work?
In your preferences pane, specify the exact topics you care about. A local AI classifier parses the daily corpus to build your custom feed.
